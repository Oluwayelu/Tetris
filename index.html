How to Play

Goal

Pieces fall from the top. Fill a complete horizontal row and it clears, dropping everything above it down. Clear 40 lines to win. If a new piece can't fit at the top, the game is over.

Scoring

ClearNamePoints
1 lineSingle100 × level
2 linesDouble300 × level
3 linesTriple500 × level
4 linesTetris800 × level

Back-to-back Tetrises score ×1.5. Consecutive clears add a combo bonus of 50 × combo × level. Four singles score 400; one Tetris scores 800 — build the well and wait for the I.

Tricks worth knowing

  • Ghost piece — the outline shows exactly where you'll land.
  • Hold — park an awkward piece and take the next one instead.
  • Lock delay — a landed piece gives you half a second to slide or rotate it before it freezes.
  • Wall kicks — rotation nudges the piece out of walls and wells, so turns that look impossible often work.
